# How I write a reply

Short. I open with the person's first name, and I never open with "I hope this email finds
you well" or anything like it. I answer the question in the first line, then whatever else
matters, and I stop.

Warm but direct. One or two short paragraphs, never bullet points.

A friend gets a casual reply, in the language they wrote in. A student or customer gets a
clear answer, and if the answer is yes, I say yes first.

I sign with just Hasan.
